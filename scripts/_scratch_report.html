02

Beyond the ask

While making the site fully editable from the dashboard, a series of audits turned up problems the client never asked about but that were actively live on the site — inherited from the platform's original template before it was set up for Trinity. Grouped below by what kind of problem each one was.

Wrong content, live on real pages

Contact page had the wrong address
Showed a Missouri address, a stranger's email, and a wrong phone number in its main contact cards. Now shows the real Odessa, TX office, phone, and email.
About Us page was a different business
Its "Capabilities" section listed decking, roofing and fencing services with matching stock photos; its mission and founder-story sections were rendering nearly blank underneath. Rewritten with real Trinity content, including a founder bio for Olin Brown.
Services page ("Why Choose Us" + FAQs)
Described a massage practice in Maryland, linked its call-to-action to a spa booking site, and answered five FAQs about deep-tissue massage. Replaced with real Trinity copy and FAQs.
FAQ page answered the wrong questions
All six FAQs were about massage therapy — one gave the business's address as an office in Maryland. Replaced with six real questions about equipment, materials, certification and location.
75 unrelated blog posts were live
Deep-tissue massage, cupping therapy, sports-massage certification — all published and feeding into "related articles" on real pages. Unpublished (not deleted); the 3 genuine Trinity posts remain live.
Homepage's social-share preview
The link preview shown when the homepage is shared on Twitter/X read "Performance Recovery Specialist Timonium." Corrected to the real Trinity description.

Content that existed but was invisible

Rod Pumps' real benefits were hidden
The page's actual, specific benefit copy was saved to a spot the page template never read, so it silently showed generic filler instead. The client's real wording was recovered and is now what displays — nothing was rewritten.
Same bug on 6 more live pages
Pipe, Valves & Fittings, Poly, Complete Facility Buildouts, General Oilfield Supply, Technical Support & Planning, and Trailer Support all had the identical problem. Fixed the same way.
Dashboard edits weren't reaching the live site
Seven service pages stored their real content in a database location the admin editor didn't write to — so an edit in the dashboard would appear to save but never show up live. Content was consolidated into the one place the editor actually manages.
FAQ tab that silently did nothing
Two product pages already had real FAQs stored elsewhere, which always overrode anything typed into a second FAQ tab — meaning that tab could never actually work for those two pages. Removed the dead-end tab.

Site behavior bugs

Navigation menu wasn't clickable
The "Artificial Lift" menu only opened its dropdown on hover — it never navigated anywhere on click. Fixed.
Nav links invisible until hovered
A site-wide styling rule defaulted all text to black; two menu items and their dropdown content were missing the one setting that keeps navbar text visible on its dark background. Fixed at the source so it can't recur elsewhere in the nav.
About page stats showing "NaN"
Three of four stat counters tried to animate text values like "USA" as numbers. Now only genuinely numeric stats animate; text values display as-is.
Broken images on About page service cards
A working image lookup was computed but never actually used — the card displayed a different, empty field instead. Rewired to use the real photo.
Quote-request form's service list was stale
Listed six old services and was missing "TAC's" entirely. Rebuilt from the live 14-item catalogue.
Hardcoded fallback booking link
Three page templates fell back to a spa-booking website's URL whenever no other link was set. Replaced with the Contact page across all three.

Design consistency

Services page didn't match the homepage
Its main listing was one long, heavy, all-dark block — a completely different visual language from the rest of the site. Rebuilt as a light card grid using the homepage's own card design, grouped into Artificial Lift and Projects & Supplies.
"Why Choose Us" section was dark, homepage version is light
Re-themed to match the light, white-background treatment used everywhere else on the site.

Dashboard guide

Everything above is now editable from /admin — no code, no developer. This is the map: which screen owns which page, and what each one lets you change.

Services (14 products) Artificial Lift page About Us Contact & FAQ Blog Photos & logos

Every product & service page

/admin/services

All 14 pages — Rod Pumps, TAC's, Sucker Rods, HD Rod Rotator, HD Plunger, and the rest of the Projects & Supplies lineup — are edited from one screen. Pick a service from the list, then move through its tabs:

OverviewHero text, stats strip, main benefit cards, photo gallery, and supplier / manufacturer logos.
Why UsThe comparison/advantage cards further down the page (used on the two Iron Bear pages).
ProcessStep-by-step section, where a page uses one.
FAQs & SupportThis page's own question-and-answer list.

Leaving a section's fields empty (benefits aside) hides that section on the live page cleanly — it won't show as a broken or empty block.

Artificial Lift landing page

/admin/pages → Artificial Lift Landing

The big-picture page above all the individual product pages.

HeroHeadline, pitch, background photo, both call-to-action buttons.
Stats BandThe four-number strip under the hero.
Why TrinityThe feature grid (icon, title, description) — add or remove cards freely.
Product IndexThe cards linking down to each product page. Order is set here, not generated automatically, so it stays predictable.
Page FAQs tabA separate tab at the top of the editor — this page's own FAQ list.

About Us

/admin/pages → About Us
Founder's StoryHeadline, description, portrait photo, and Olin's bio, quote and contact details.
MissionHeadline, description, and the four principle cards ("USA-Made Materials," etc.)
StatsThe four-number impact strip.
CapabilitiesSection heading, plus which services to feature as cards — pick from the real 14-item catalogue, or leave unset to show the top 6 automatically.
Commitments / Why Choose / TeamAlready correct and Trinity-specific — edit freely, no known issues.

Contact & FAQ pages

/admin/pages → Contact Center · Support FAQ
Contact infoPhone, email, address and hours shown on the Contact page's info cards — this is the block that had the wrong Missouri address; it's correct now, editable here going forward.
Contact headerThe badge, headline and intro line at the top of the Contact page.
FAQ pageIts own separate question list, distinct from any single service page's FAQs.

Blog

/admin → Posts

Three real Trinity posts are live today. The 75 unrelated posts were unpublished, not deleted, so they can be reviewed or permanently removed later if wanted.

Open Posts in the admin sidebar and click New Post.
Fill in title, body, and a featured image, then set status to Published when ready.
Published posts appear automatically in the "Oilfield Insights" section on the homepage and About page.

Photos & supplier logos

Any editor field with an image box

Every photo and logo field across the dashboard works the same way: click the image box, upload a file, done. On the Overview tab of any service, the Supplier Logos section also lets you choose Dark chip or Light chip per logo, so a logo with dark artwork (like TechTAC) and one with light artwork (like Percheron) both stay legible.
